Padma Shri nomination profile

A structured public profile gathered in support of the 2024 Padma Shri nomination of Guru Ramakanta Mahapatra.

A nomination proposes a name for India’s civilian honour. It is not the conferment of the Padma Shri. This page does not describe him as a Padma Shri awardee.

The journey to Japan

In 1995 the fifteen-foot Ashokan pillar was installed in Japan, with Ramakanta named as a main artisan beside his elder brother. In 1998 he carved for Mr. Abnor a nine-foot chain and a fifteen-foot corner in red Khandolite and green serpentine. Later work includes a Radha Krishna and a Bal Ganesh for the Ashutosh Muni Foundation at Walkers Creek Road in the United States, a Ganesha and a Navagraha sent to Italy in 2013, a Ganesha for the Pandara Ganesh Museum in Thailand, figures for a museum in Moscow, and the seven temple shrines in Singapore.

National recognition

On 14 March 1997 the Orissa Lalit Kala Akademi honoured the sculpture Puja Ganesha, judged best at the 25th All Orissa Art Exhibition. In 2005 the Ministry of Textiles conferred the National Award for Master Craftsperson in stone carving, the honour from which the title National Awardee proceeds. He received it from the President of India, Smt. Pratibha Devisingh Patil. In 2008 STONA in Bengaluru gave him first prize for stone carving. In 2014 he received the Silpi Kala Bhaskara title for a sculptural dome, and in 2015 the Nitya Nutan Samman from the State Excise Minister, Devi Prasad Mishra. Singapore, and a long run of certificates of appreciation, sit beside those honours.

A thousand artisans, and colour held in stone

More than a thousand artisans have trained under his guidance. He has treated teaching as a duty to the craftsmen’s community: skill kept in many hands, not held as a private secret. He is also known for multi-coloured stone patchwork, composing one image from fitted stones of differing colour and grain so that the sculpture holds both structural unity and a jeweled surface.

The Padma Shri nomination

In 2024 the record of temple work, international installation and artisan training was gathered for a Padma Shri nomination. A nomination proposes a name for India’s civilian honour. It is not itself the conferment of the award. This site presents the public record that accompanies that proposal, and it does not describe him as a Padma Shri recipient.

Mahapatra Handicrafts Emporium, a unit of P.R. Exports. Stone carving in Bhubaneswar, Odisha. Nuagaon, Gangotri Nagar.
