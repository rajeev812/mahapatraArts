Awards and recognitions

The honour wall

State, national and institutional recognitions, together with international notice. Certificate scans from the archive appear in the frames when they are placed.

  1. 1990

    Odisha State Award

    State Best Designing Award, 1990. The first major public recognition of design as well as carving.

    Mahapatra Arts Odisha State Award 1990
  2. 1997

    Lalit Kala Academy Award

    The Orissa State Lalit Kala Akademi Award, dated 14 March 1997, for the sculpture Puja Ganesha, adjudged best at the 25th All Orissa Art Exhibition.

  3. 2005

    National Award for Master Craftsperson

    The Ministry of Textiles certificate of 2005 names Ramakanta Mahapatra for stone carving. He received the National Award for Master Craftsperson from the President of India, Smt. Pratibha Devisingh Patil. This is the honour named when the atelier says National Awardee.

  4. 2008

    STONA first prize

    First prize at STONA 2008, the 8th International Granites and Stone Fair in Bengaluru, for outstanding stone carving. The stone trophy is inscribed by the All India Granites and Stone Association, and the framed appreciation names Mr. Ramakanta Mahapatra.

  5. —

    Guru Samrat Award

    An honour that names the teacher, not only the carver.

    Mahapatra Arts Guru Samrat Award —
  6. 2014

    Silpi Kala Bhaskara

    The Silpi Kala Bhaskara title in 2014, at the silver jubilee of Netra Utsav Bhajana Samaroh, Sri Jagannath Kala Mahavidyalaya, Puri, for his contribution to a sculptural dome.

    Mahapatra Arts Silpi Kala Bhaskara 2014
  7. 2015

    Nitya Nutan Samman

    The Nitya Nutan Samman in 2015, from the State Excise Minister, Devi Prasad Mishra.

    Mahapatra Arts Nitya Nutan Samman 2015
  8. —

    International recognition, Singapore

    The Sri Krishnan Temple, 152 Waterloo Street, Singapore, certified the seven temple shrines, one Perumal Mandapam in Indian red onyx, and other black onyx work. P. Sivaraman, Chairman of the Board of Trustees, signed the certificate. The project ran from 13 February 2015 to 15 May 2018.

  9. —

    Certificates of appreciation

    Letters from the Kripalu Samadhi Mandir, A. K. Das Associates, Canixa Life Sciences, Gopalan Enterprises for a Radha Krishna in 2018, Dr. Vishwanath D. Karad of MIT-WPU Pune, Dr. D. C. Ebinezar for Viswaroop Darsan, the Bangalore Gourakshan Shala for a Ganesh, and the Faridabad Industries Association in 2000.

Padma Shri nomination profile

Mahapatra Handicrafts Emporium, a unit of P.R. Exports. Stone carving in Bhubaneswar, Odisha. Nuagaon, Gangotri Nagar.
