A walk through six rooms

A quiet sequence through the practice: threshold, sacred image, temple, the world, the teaching floor, and the honour wall. Use the room index or scroll.

01 Threshold 02 Sacred image 03 Temple 04 The world 05 Teaching floor 06 Honour wall

Room 01

Threshold

You enter as a visitor enters a temple compound: slowly, and by a gate. The atelier’s gate work, including the large heritage gateway in Odisha, is the idea of this room.

View

Room 02

Sacred image

Ganesha, Krishna, Jagannath, the Dasavatara and Viswaroop. The image is not decoration of a religion. It is the religion’s proportion, cut in stone.

View

Room 03

Temple

Pillars, mandapams, wheels and shrines. Architectural stone is judged by whether a building can trust it.

View

Room 04

The world

Japan in 1995, Singapore’s seven shrines, and the wider map of eight countries.

View

Room 05

Teaching floor

More than a thousand artisans. The academy keeps that duty open to students from outside India.

View

Room 06

Honour wall

1990, 1997, 2005, and the 2024 nomination, which remains a nomination.
