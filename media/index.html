The public record

Interviews, news, documentaries, the Padma Shri nomination story and exhibitions. Clippings are filed here as the archive is assembled. The chapters below are the record this site can state today.

Sri Krishnan Temple, Singapore

The temple’s certificate for seven shrines, one Perumal Mandapam in Indian red onyx, and other black onyx work at 152 Waterloo Street. Signed by P. Sivaraman. The work ran from 13 February 2015 to 15 May 2018.

Padma Shri nomination · 2024

The 2024 nomination story

In 2024 the atelier gathered its record — temple work, international installation, and the training of artisans — for a Padma Shri nomination. The nomination profile on this site is that gathering. It is a proposal for honour, not the honour itself.
